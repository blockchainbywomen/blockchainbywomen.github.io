04

report

Report to law enforcement, with the help of bAIW if needed. If you think you have been scammed, act quickly and keep the evidence.

  • Save wallet addresses, transaction IDs, dates and amounts, and screenshots of every message, profile and website involved.
  • Tell your bank, exchange or payment app straight away.
  • File a report with the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov and with the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
  • Be careful with anyone who contacts you offering to recover lost funds for a fee. That is often a second scam.

We investigate and assess potential fraud. If you are looking for help, email us at blockchainbywomen@gmail.com.

prevent loss

We use AI to find the gaps between what happened and what was recorded, and hand the evidence to a person.

Loss prevention, built safely

Most loss is not a dramatic theft. It is a small gap between what happened and what was recorded. Those gaps hide in plain sight because nobody has time to check every record against reality.

This is our applied security work: AI that compares what was seen with what the system of record says, and surfaces the differences for a person to verify.

We built it the way we inform: transparent about what it checks and careful with data. No one is identified. Results are verified.
