Role of AI in Finance in 2025

As AI continues to redefine the financial sector, new opportunities—and challenges—are emerging at an unprecedented pace. In this session, leaders from Stripe, Ramp, Crypto Conexion come together to explore the transformative impact of AI on finance in 2025. From improving transaction security and fraud detection to stabilizing the value of digital assets and stablecoins, AI’s role in shaping a resilient and inclusive financial future is just beginning.

read more

Join us for an in-depth panel discussion covering:

  • AI and Financial Security: Discover how cutting-edge AI solutions are enhancing cybersecurity in payments, lending, and transactions while introducing new challenges in fraud prevention and data privacy.
  • Stablecoins and Digital Currencies: Examine AI's potential to bolster the reliability and security of stablecoins, paving the way for increased adoption across traditional and decentralized finance.
  • Responsible AI Implementation: Delve into the ethical and regulatory considerations surrounding AI in finance, from data transparency to responsible AI deployment for financial inclusion.

Panelists:

  • Stacey Wong, Stripe
  • Amanda Carroccio, Ramp
  • Karen Hsu, Appdome and Blockchain by Women
  • Monica Talan, CryptoConnection

read more

  • What new stable coins are coming out in 2020 and how are they different

Blockchain in Energy: Use Cases and Projects

Karen Hsu, co-founder of Blockchain By Women, kicked off the event by giving us an overview of the current market trends moving from centralized to decentralized, with DERs penetration. She shared a P2P transaction test example for enabling distributed energy markets and facilitating increased penetration of DER in the retail utility market.

read more

On our panelist, we heard from Lydia Kreft on how they're using the blockchain to give people using EV charging stations the credits they are due based on time of day of charging and other factors. Sherry Li, from GE, gave her perspective on the blockchain opportunity in Energy, while Rena Shah shared with us her experience using the blockchain to address energy crises during national disasters in California and other parts of the U.S. View the livestream here.
