about

bAIW (Blockchain and AI by Women) helps consumers understand how to protect themselves and their assets, and helps builders create systems people can trust.

We started as Blockchain by Women, created to raise awareness, educate and connect people in a fast-growing space. As blockchain matured, awareness was no longer enough. People needed practical ways to stay safe, and the teams shipping products needed to build them securely. AI now raises the same questions of security, risk and trust, and we bring the same approach to it.

We are a community for everyone that leads and empowers, including:

  • Helping consumers understand the risks and how to protect themselves and their assets
  • Educating on AI agent integrity and trust in systems built using AI
  • Providing technology-based loss prevention services
  • Investigating and assessing potential fraud
  • Showcasing the work and expertise of builders through curated panels, online and in person

Karen Hsu

Founder, bAIW

With 30 years in technology companies, Karen is co-inventor of 5 patents and for over 25 years has built and taken to market software for financial institutions and fintechs to better manage and serve their customers. She worked with financial standards organizations including SWIFT and ACORD to create global standards for exchanging data on financial transactions among financial institutions, companies, agents and other stakeholders.

Karen co-founded BlockchainIntel to prevent fraud in blockchain, ACH and credit card transactions and transitioned the company to become part of Appdome to expand the mission of protecting everyone using mobile apps. At Appdome, Karen led go-to-market teams protecting against AI-driven fraud, and worked with DevOps vendors and with fintechs that process trillions of dollars in payments annually to secure their mobile apps.

Karen started Blocksteer to protect against AI-driven fraud and ensure the integrity of AI infrastructure.

She partners with law enforcement agencies around the world such as the FBI to identify and stop bad actors who have stolen digital assets from individuals. Partnering with professional organizations such as ACAMS, she has shared the work she and her teams have done so they can better combat money laundering and terrorist financing.

Karen co-founded and has been on the advisory board of BASES (Business Association for Stanford Entrepreneurial Students), one of the largest student-run organizations, which hosts an annual business plan contest, educational series and workshops for students. BASES students and alumni have founded fintechs and other companies.

Karen co-founded Blockchain by Women, now bAIW, to help consumers protect themselves and help everyone build safely in blockchain and AI. She has since extended bAIW to provide technology-based loss prevention services, with education focused on AI agent integrity and trust in systems built using AI.
