Blockchain by Women now works across blockchain and AI. The identity says so in one move: the letters **AI**, already inside the word blockchain, are capitalised, set in purple and held in square brackets: blockch[AI]n. Everything else stays black, white and square.

## The name

- Write the full name **blockch[AI]n by women**: all lowercase, capital A and I inside square brackets, no other capitals, even at the start of a sentence or in a headline.
- Write the short name **b[AI]w**: lowercase b and w, capital A and I inside square brackets. Use it once the full name has appeared, and for handles, hashtags and the app icon.
- Colour the brackets and the AI together wherever colour is available: `signal` in logos and headlines, `signal-text` in running text and email. The AI is weight 700; the brackets match the surrounding letters.
- Where there is no colour (plain text), the brackets and capitals carry it: blockch[AI]n, b[AI]w. Where brackets are not allowed (URLs, handles, hashtags, file names), drop them and keep the capitals: blockchAIn, bAIw.
- Bracket only the AI in the name. Do not bracket or capitalise "ai" inside other words (chair, training).
- When speaking, say "blockchain by women". The AI is seen, not pronounced.

## Tagline

- The tagline is **Building and Using Blockchain and AI Safely**. Write it exactly so, in title case, with no full stop; it is the one line that is not lowercase.
- Set it in `lead` or `label` under the name, in `ink` or `muted`. Do not bracket or colour the AI in the tagline; the brackets belong to the name.
- Use `baiw-tagline-*.svg` when the logo and tagline appear together; do not typeset the pair by hand.

- Clear space: keep at least the height of the lowercase "o" free on every side. The files already include it; do not crop them.

- Put the colour logo only on `paper`, `blush` or white; the reverse logo only on `ink` or a photo dark enough to hold white text. On `signal`, use the `-white` file.
- Do not recolour the bracketed AI, fill a box behind it, tilt it, add shadows or gradients, stretch the logo, or change the spacing around the brackets.
- Do not put a second highlight colour anywhere near the logo.

## Colour

- Build pages from `paper` and `ink`. Roughly nine parts black and white to one part purple.
- Spend `signal` once per view: the logo's [AI] or one highlighted block in a pattern. If two things are `signal`, one of them should not be.
- Set text in `ink`; secondary text in `muted`. Set links in `signal-text`, underlined.
- `signal` is for the logo, 24px+ lettering and blocks. For small purple text use `signal-text`, on `paper` only. On a `signal` ground put only the white logo or 24px+ `on-signal` lettering.
- Use `blush` for a quiet band or callout when a page needs relief from white. Use `line` for hairlines only.
- In the dark theme `paper` and `ink` swap. `signal` stays exactly the same; only small purple text (`signal-text`) gets paler.

## Type

- Set everything in Jost (`font-sans`); the file is in `fonts/`. Fall back to Futura, then Century Gothic.
- Headlines (`display`, `h1`, `h2`): weight 500, lowercase, no full stop. "women building what comes next".
- `lead` at weight 300 under a headline; `body` at 400 for running text, in sentence case with normal punctuation.
- `label` for buttons, tags and dates: lowercase, weight 600, tracked 0.08em.
- Use weight 700 only for the AI in the name. Do not use italics for emphasis; use weight 500.
- Left-align. Centre only a single headline on a poster or title slide.

## Shape and layout

- Everything is square: `radius-none` on blocks, buttons, cards and photos. `radius-full` only for portrait avatars.
- The motif is a chain of square blocks on a `space-4` (48px) grid with exactly one block in `signal`: the AI in the chain. Square brackets in `signal` may also frame a single word or number in a headline, never more than one per view. Other blocks are `ink`, `blush` or `line`. Use it on covers, section dividers and social cards; do not use it behind text.
- No gradients, no shadows, no glows, no circuit-board or robot imagery.
- Buttons: `ink` fill, `paper` `label` text, `space-2` padding, square. Secondary buttons: 2px `ink` border, `ink` text, no fill. Buttons are never purple; the purple belongs to the [AI].
- Focus ring: 2px solid `ink`, offset 2px (in dark: white).
- Photos: real members and events, in colour, cropped square or 3:2, no filters or colour overlays.

## Voice

- Plain, warm and direct. Speak as "we" to "you".
- Lead with what people will do or learn, then the technology. "Learn to ship a smart contract in a weekend", not "Leveraging decentralised paradigms".
- Name blockchain and AI as equal subjects; do not present AI as an add-on.
- Safety is the promise in the tagline: say what makes a practice safe (audits, key custody, data privacy, model evaluation) rather than only claiming it.
- No emoji in headlines or on the logo. No hype words (revolutionary, disrupt, 10x) and no exclamation marks in headlines.
